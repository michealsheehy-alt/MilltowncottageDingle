Stay together in West Kerry

Family accommodation in Dingle with space for eight.

Four bedrooms, a private garden and practical home comforts make Milltown Cottage an easy base for a family holiday on the Dingle Peninsula.

A shared home

More flexible than booking several hotel rooms.

Milltown Cottage lets a family or multigenerational group stay under one roof while still having room to spread out. Four bedrooms provide flexible sleeping arrangements, and separate living areas give adults and children space for different rhythms.

The kitchen makes early breakfasts, packed lunches and relaxed dinners straightforward. Laundry facilities are useful during a longer trip, while private parking removes the daily question of where to leave the car.

Days everyone can enjoy

Family things to do around Dingle.

The peninsula offers simple outdoor days, engaging wet-weather options and scenery that does not require a strenuous hike.

Dingle Oceanworld

The aquarium is approximately an 11-minute walk from the cottage and is a useful family option in any weather.

Beaches

Inch and Ventry offer broad sandy spaces for walking, paddling, surfing and unhurried family afternoons.

Scenic exploring

Slea Head Drive, Dunquin and the Blasket viewpoints create a memorable day with plenty of short stops.

Meals made easier

Cook at home or eat in town.

A fully equipped kitchen means you are not committed to restaurant meals for every breakfast and dinner. When you do want to go out, Dingle has casual seafood, cafés, ice cream and welcoming pubs and restaurants.

Location

Quiet when you need it, close when you do not.

The walk into Dingle town takes approximately 15 minutes, and central Dingle is about five minutes away by car. The harbour, shops and attractions are close, but the cottage remains surrounded by the open landscape and changing light of West Kerry.
