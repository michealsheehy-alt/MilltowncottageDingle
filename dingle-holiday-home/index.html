Self-catering accommodation in Dingle

A four-bedroom Dingle holiday home with room to breathe.

Milltown Cottage sleeps up to eight guests in a peaceful setting close to Dingle town, with generous living spaces, a private garden and views across West Kerry.

The property

Space for families and friends to settle in.

Milltown Cottage is designed for the kind of holiday where everyone can spend time together without feeling crowded. The open kitchen and dining area makes breakfast, long dinners and planning the next day easy. Separate living areas provide quieter corners for reading, working or simply taking a break.

The house combines the independence of self-catering accommodation with a location close to Dingle’s harbour, restaurants, pubs and shops. After a day on Slea Head Drive or the beaches of the peninsula, you can return to a calm home away from the busiest streets.

Why stay here

Close to Dingle, with the quiet of the peninsula around you.

The walk from Milltown Cottage into Dingle town takes approximately 15 minutes, while central Dingle is about five minutes away by car. The cottage is well positioned for the harbour, the Wild Atlantic Way, Ventry Beach, Slea Head, Conor Pass and the wider Dingle Peninsula.

Guests can cook at home, walk toward town for dinner or use the cottage as a comfortable base for full days exploring West Kerry. The private garden and on-site parking make arrivals and unhurried mornings especially easy.
