Dingle to Adare Manor

How far is Dingle from Adare Manor?

Milltown Cottage is approximately 133 km / 83 miles from Adare Manor. In normal traffic, the drive takes about two hours, generally travelling from Dingle toward Tralee on the N86 and then east toward Adare on the N21.

Ryder Cup traffic and official access controls are likely to make event-day travel longer. A private driver can be arranged for return travel, but the journey will still follow the tournament’s official park-and-ride and shuttle system rather than direct venue drop-off.

When and where is the 2027 Ryder Cup?

The 2027 Ryder Cup takes place at Adare Manor in County Limerick from 13–19 September 2027. Build-up days run from 13–16 September and competition between Europe and the United States runs from Friday 17 to Sunday 19 September.

How far is Milltown Cottage from Adare Manor?

Milltown Cottage is approximately 133 km / 83 miles from Adare Manor. The drive takes about two hours in normal traffic. Tournament traffic and official access controls are likely to add time.

Is a private driver available for Ryder Cup days?

Yes. A private driver is available by prior arrangement, subject to availability and the Ryder Cup's official transport rules. Guests travelling by road will use the official park-and-ride system rather than direct venue drop-off.

How many guests can Milltown Cottage accommodate?

Milltown Cottage sleeps up to eight guests in four bedrooms: one king bedroom, two double bedrooms and one twin bedroom. The cottage has two bathrooms.

Are Ryder Cup tickets included?

No. Accommodation and driver arrangements do not include Ryder Cup tickets. Guests must secure admission separately through official Ryder Cup channels.
