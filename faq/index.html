Before your stay

Frequently asked questions about Milltown Cottage.

Practical answers about the house, booking, house rules, parking and travelling to Dingle.

The cottage

Space, facilities and location.

If your question is not answered here, email michealsheehy@gmail.com.

How many guests can Milltown Cottage accommodate?

The cottage accommodates up to eight guests across four bedrooms and has two bathrooms. It includes generous living areas, a fully equipped kitchen, laundry facilities and a private garden.

What is the bedroom layout?

There is a principal king bedroom, two additional double bedrooms and a twin bedroom with two single beds.

How far is the cottage from Dingle town?

The walk from Milltown Cottage into Dingle town takes approximately 15 minutes. Central Dingle is also about five minutes away by car.

Is Milltown Cottage suitable for families?

Yes. Four bedrooms, two bathrooms, shared living spaces, a kitchen, laundry facilities, a private garden and proximity to Dingle make it a practical family base.

Is parking available?

Yes. The cottage has free private parking for up to four cars.

Does the cottage have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Fast Wi-Fi is available throughout Milltown Cottage.

What is included for a self-catering stay?

The cottage includes a fully equipped kitchen, dining and living areas, bed linens, bathrooms, laundry facilities, a private garden, parking and self check-in.

Are pets permitted?

No. Guests are not permitted to bring pets or other animals to the property.

Getting here

Airport and public transport information.

Always check live transport information before travelling because schedules can change. American guests can also read our USA travel guide.

What is the closest airport to Milltown Cottage?

Kerry Airport is the closest airport to Milltown Cottage and Dingle.

Which airport should I use when travelling from the United States?

Kerry Airport is closest to Dingle. Shannon and Cork are useful regional gateways, while Dublin offers the widest choice of international flights. The best option depends on your route and wider Ireland itinerary.

Can I travel to and from Dingle with Bus Éireann?

Yes. Bus Éireann Route 275 operates between Dingle and Tralee via Camp. Tralee provides connections for onward travel. Check the current timetable before setting out.

Do I need a car in Dingle?

A car is useful for exploring the wider peninsula, beaches and scenic drives, but the cottage is approximately a 15-minute walk from Dingle town and Bus Éireann connects Dingle with Tralee.

Where can I check live bus information?

Use the official Bus Éireann Route 275 page for the current timetable and stops.

What power adapter do American visitors need?

Ireland uses Type G three-pin plugs. Bring a suitable adapter and confirm that personal electrical devices support the local voltage before use.

Ryder Cup 2027

Staying in Dingle for golf’s centenary event.

Read our Ryder Cup 2027 accommodation guide for full distance, transport and cottage details. For booking and driver enquiries, email michealsheehy@gmail.com with your preferred dates and group size.

When and where is the 2027 Ryder Cup?

The Ryder Cup takes place at Adare Manor in County Limerick from 13–19 September 2027. Build-up days run from Monday 13 to Thursday 16 September, followed by Europe v USA competition from Friday 17 to Sunday 19 September.

How far is Milltown Cottage from Adare Manor?

The road journey is approximately 133 km / 83 miles and takes around two hours in normal traffic. Tournament traffic and access controls are likely to add time, so guests should follow current event guidance and leave well in advance.

Can a private driver be arranged?

Yes. A private driver can be arranged for return travel on Ryder Cup days, subject to availability and the tournament’s official transport arrangements. Spectators travelling by road will use official park-and-ride locations and dedicated shuttles rather than being dropped directly at the venue.

How do I book Ryder Cup dates?

Please email michealsheehy@gmail.com with your preferred dates and group size. Ryder Cup tickets are not included with accommodation and must be secured separately through official ticket channels.

Where can I check event transport arrangements?

Use the Ryder Cup’s official transport guidance for current park-and-ride, shuttle and access information. Final arrangements should be checked again shortly before travelling.

Is Milltown Cottage affiliated with the Ryder Cup or Adare Manor?

No. Milltown Cottage is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ryder Cup or Adare Manor.
