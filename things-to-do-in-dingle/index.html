Your West Kerry itinerary

Things to do in Dingle and around the peninsula.

From harbour mornings and traditional music to Atlantic drives, ancient sites and sandy beaches, Dingle rewards every kind of day.

Start in town

Give Dingle time to unfold.

Dingle — Daingean Uí Chúis — is a working harbour town at the heart of Corca Dhuibhne. Begin with a walk along the waterfront, explore independent shops and galleries, then pause for coffee or ice cream before the evening music begins. Irish language, fishing, farming and storytelling remain part of daily life rather than something staged for visitors.

Milltown Cottage keeps you close to that energy while providing a quieter base at the end of the day. The walk into Dingle town takes approximately 15 minutes, while central Dingle is about five minutes away by car.

Essential experiences

Four unforgettable Dingle days.

Opening times and seasonal services can change, so confirm details with individual operators before setting out.

Slea Head & Dunquin

Follow the peninsula’s signature coastal drive past Ventry, stone forts, Atlantic viewpoints and the winding path at Dunquin Pier.

Plan the drive

Ancient West Kerry

Visit Gallarus Oratory and explore a landscape rich in early Christian sites, ring forts and archaeology.

Beach day

Walk, surf or swim at Inch Beach, or travel west to the clear water and broad sands of Ventry Bay.

Conor Pass

Take Ireland’s high mountain road north from Dingle for waterfalls and sweeping views across the peninsula.

Harbour & town

Browse the colourful streets, watch the fishing boats and enjoy a town small enough to explore on foot.

Walks & wildlife

Choose a harbour stroll, a section of the Dingle Way, a Mount Brandon hike or a boat trip toward the Blasket Islands.

For an easy day

Family-friendly Dingle.

Dingle Oceanworld Aquarium is close to the cottage and works well on a wet-weather morning. Boat trips, beaches and shorter harbour walks are good choices for mixed-age groups, while the cottage’s kitchen and garden make it easy to return for a quieter afternoon.

After dark

Music, pubs and conversation.

Traditional music is part of Dingle’s identity. Dick Mack’s, Foxy John’s, Curran’s, Kennedy’s and The Dingle Pub each offer a different atmosphere, from intimate old rooms to lively evening entertainment.
