Privacy and cookie policy.

This page explains how information is handled when you visit the Milltown Cottage Dingle website or contact us about a stay.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this website. Analytics is optional and is loaded only after you select “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner.

Google Analytics may process information such as the pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source and interactions with booking or contact links. We use this information in aggregate to improve the website and understand interest in Milltown Cottage.

Your cookie choice

Your analytics preference is stored in your browser so the website can remember your choice. Declining analytics does not prevent you from browsing the site, checking availability or contacting us.

Booking and third-party services

Live availability and direct checkout are provided by Lodgify. If you continue to Lodgify or Airbnb, those services process information under their own privacy and cookie policies. This website also links to maps, local businesses and transport providers.

Email enquiries

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, manage a reservation or discuss a potential stay. Please avoid sending payment card information by email.

Contact

For a privacy question or request concerning information you have provided directly, email michealsheehy@gmail.com.

You can also review Google’s privacy policy.
